# Method Kernel

*   **Purpose**
    *   The GROW model clarifies the current situation of a coachee and aims to enhance awareness and responsibility for performance improvement and learning. [EXTRACTED] "clarify the current situation" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, 11 R: What Is Reality?, p1) "essential nature of awareness and responsibility for learning and for performance improvement." (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, 9 The GROW Model, p1)
    *   Specifically, the "G" stage defines goals for the session and the overall issue, distinguishing between end, performance, dream, and process goals. [EXTRACTED] "defining various goals" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, 11 R: What Is Reality?, p1) "The goal for the session" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, The goal for the session, p1)
    *   The "R" stage examines reality objectively to bring goals into sharper focus. [EXTRACTED] "examining reality is objectivity" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Objectivity, p1) "brings the goals into sharper focus" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, 11 R: What Is Reality?, p1)
    *   The "O" stage generates numerous alternative courses of action without immediate judgment, expanding creative possibilities. [EXTRACTED] "purpose of the options stage in GROW is not to find the 'right' answer, but to create and list as many alternative courses of action as possible." (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, 12 O: What Options Do You Have?, p1)

*   **Preconditions**
    *   Effective use of the GROW model requires a context of awareness and responsibility, alongside the skill of active listening and powerful questioning. [EXTRACTED] "GROW has little value without the context of awareness and responsibility and the intention and skill to generate them through active listening and powerful questions." (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, More than GROW, p1)
    *   For the reality phase, objectivity and detachment are crucial to bypass distortions from opinions, judgments, and fears. [EXTRACTED] "most important criterion for examining reality is objectivity." (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Objectivity, p1) "demands a high degree of detachment on the part of the coach" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Detachment, p1)
    *   Generating options necessitates a safe environment where participants feel comfortable expressing ideas without inhibition or fear of judgment. [EXTRACTED] "create an environment in which participants will feel safe enough to express their thoughts and ideas without inhibition or fear of judgment" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Maximizing choices, p1)

[EXTRACTED] "attitude of the coach" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, 9 The GROW Model, p1) "demands factual answers from the coachee." *   **Process**
    *   The GROW model outlines a four-stage sequence: Goal, Reality, Options, and Will. [EXTRACTED] "sequence of questions I suggest follows four distinct stages" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, A framework for coaching, p3)
    *   The sequence is flexible; while it typically begins with Goal, coaching can start and end at any stage, especially for ongoing tasks. [EXTRACTED] "framework is flexible." (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, A framework for coaching, p5) "coaching may begin and end with any stage." (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, A framework for coaching, p5)
    *   **Goal Setting (G):** Identifies ultimate, performance, and progress goals, clarifies aspirations, and defines desired session outcomes. Goals should be Specific, Measurable, Agreed, Realistic, Timeframed (SMART); Positively stated, Understood, Relevant, Ethical (PURE); and Challenging, Legal, Environmentally sound, Appropriate, Recorded (CLEAR). [EXTRACTED] "STEP 1: WHAT ARE YOUR GOALS?" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, STEP 1: WHAT ARE YOUR GOALS?, p1) "goals need to be SMART" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Qualities of a good goal, p1) "and also PURE" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Qualities of a good goal, p2) "and CLEAR" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Qualities of a good goal, p3)
    *   **Reality Checking (R):** Assesses the current situation by reviewing past actions and their effects, clarifying internal and external obstacles. It involves deep awareness, focusing on facts through "what, when, where, who, how much" questions, and encouraging descriptive rather than judgmental language. [EXTRACTED] "STEP 2: WHAT IS THE REALITY?" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, STEP 2: WHAT IS THE REALITY?, p1) "most often be initiated by the interrogatives 'what,' 'when,' 'where,' 'who,' and 'how much.'" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Reality questions, p3) "descriptive not judgmental" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Reality questions, p2)
    *   **Options (O):** Identifies and lists a variety of strategies and alternatives. This stage emphasizes quantity over quality initially, encouraging creative thinking by challenging negative assumptions and asking "What if...?" questions. Brainstorming and exploring metaphors are also used to expand possibilities. [EXTRACTED] "STEP 3: WHAT ARE YOUR OPTIONS?" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, STEP 3: WHAT ARE YOUR OPTIONS?, p1) "quantity of options is more important at this stage than the quality and feasibility" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, 12 O: What Options Do You Have?, p1) "What if there were no obstacles, what would you do then?" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Negative assumptions, p2)
    *   The model allows for "recycling" where it may be necessary to revisit earlier stages (e.g., redefine goals after examining reality). [EXTRACTED] "subject to recycling." (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, Context and flexibility, p1)

*   **Decision Rules**
    *   Coaches should prioritize flexibility and intuition when moving through the GROW sequence rather than adhering to rigid rules. [EXTRACTED] "Follow your own intuition and instinct rather than trying to obey a rule." (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, Context and flexibility, p5)
    *   Goals should be inspiring and stretching for the coachee, and meet SMART, PURE, and CLEAR criteria. [EXTRACTED] "goal which is both inspirational and stretching for them" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, The key to GROW, p1) "goals need to be SMART" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Qualities of a good goal, p1)
    *   In the reality phase, use "what," "when," "where," "who," and "how much" questions, avoiding "how" and "why" to prevent analysis, opinion, or defensiveness. [EXTRACTED] "most often be initiated by the interrogatives 'what,' 'when,' 'where,' 'who,' and 'how much.'" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Reality questions, p3) "'how' and 'why' should be used only sparingly" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Reality questions, p3)
    *   For options, all contributions should be noted without censorship to maximize choices, and critical evaluation (benefits/costs) comes later. [EXTRACTED] "All contributions, however apparently silly, need to be noted down" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Maximizing choices, p1) "the will phase of coaching may just be a simple matter of selecting the best" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Sorting options BENEFITS AND COSTS, p1)

*   **Success Signals**
    *   Coachee sets inspirational and stretching goals. [EXTRACTED] "coachee sets a goal which is both inspirational and stretching for them" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, The key to GROW, p1)
    *   Achieving success through challenging goals increases self-belief and confidence. [EXTRACTED] "inspirational goal which is also challenging will result in success, which increases self-belief and confidence" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Inspiring goals, p2)
    *   Coachee gains new awareness and is empowered by it. [EXTRACTED] "new awareness becomes conscious, and the coachee is empowered by it." (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Deeper awareness, p1)
    *   Thorough investigation of reality leads to early solutions or "Eureka!" moments. [EXTRACTED] "thorough investigation of reality throws up the answer before you even enter the third and fourth stages" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Early resolution, p1)
    *   Creative thought is unleashed, and obstacles are found to be less insurmountable. [EXTRACTED] "more creative thought is unleashed and perhaps the obstacle is found to be less insurmountable" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Negative assumptions, p2)

*   **Failure Modes & Fixes**
    *   **Failure Mode:** Goals based solely on current reality, leading to negative, limited, or problem-reactive goals. [EXTRACTED] "goals based on current reality alone are liable to be negative, a response to a problem" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, Goal first, p1)
    *   **Fix:** Formulate goals by identifying an ideal long-term vision first, then determine realistic steps. [EXTRACTED] "Goals formed by ascertaining the ideal long-term solution or vision, and then determining realistic steps toward that ideal, are generally far more inspiring" (016_9_the_grow_model_with_descriptions.md, Goal first, p2)
    *   **Failure Mode:** Leaders imposing goals, denying ownership, or using negative framing. [EXTRACTED] "This denies ownership to those who are expected to meet these targets, and their performance is likely to suffer accordingly." (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Ownership of goals, p1) "If you fear failure, you are focused on failure and that is what you get." (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Inspiring goals, p3)
    *   **Fix:** Coach for ownership, allow coachees to set or refine goals, and convert negative goals to positive ones. [EXTRACTED] "it is still possible to coach for ownership" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, COACHING FOR OWNERSHIP, p1) "Negative goals can easily be converted into the positive opposite" (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, Positive focus, p1)
    *   **Failure Mode:** Distortions of reality due to opinions, judgments, or "false reality syndrome." [EXTRACTED] "Objectivity is subject to major distortions caused by the opinions, judgments, expectations, prejudices, concerns, hopes, and fears" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Objectivity, p1) "blame culture...causes 'false reality syndrome'" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Direct communication among peers, p4)
    *   **Fix:** Maintain detachment, use descriptive language, and probe for deeper awareness by encouraging coachees to stop and think. [EXTRACTED] "Demands a high degree of detachment" (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Detachment, p1) "When the coachee has to stop to think before responding...their awareness is being raised." (018_11_r_what_is_reality_with_descriptions.md, Deeper awareness, p1)
    *   **Failure Mode:** Self-limiting assumptions restricting creative solutions. [EXTRACTED] "factors that most restricts the generation of creative solutions...is the implicit assumptions we carry" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Negative assumptions, p1)
    *   **Fix:** Challenge assumptions with "What if...?" questions, use metaphors, and brainstorm broadly. [EXTRACTED] "What if there were no obstacles, what would you do then?" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Negative assumptions, p2) "ask the person to create a metaphor" (019_12_o_what_options_do_you_have_with_descriptions.md, Expanding creativity, p3)
