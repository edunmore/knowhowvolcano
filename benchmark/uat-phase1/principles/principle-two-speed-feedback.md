# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
Feedback in a learning unit should be delivered in two layers: a fast, memorable [[Rule of Thumb]] for use during live action, followed by a slow, deeper explanation of why the rule works and when it fails.

## Rationale
The fast layer prevents [[Overthinking]] during a live conversation, enabling performance. The slow layer prevents [[Blind Habit]] by providing the underlying rationale, which aids in [[Calibration]] and [[Transfer]]. This approach respects the [[Friction Budget]] by structuring information for deliberate practice without overwhelming the learner.
