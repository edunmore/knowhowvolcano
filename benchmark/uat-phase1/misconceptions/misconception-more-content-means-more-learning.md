# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The mistaken belief that adding more examples, more theory, or more content to a learning unit will automatically increase the amount a learner retains and can apply. Teams often operate under this assumption, believing it will improve learning outcomes.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Does this stem from a confusion between information exposure and skill development? (2) Is it a default response when transfer is not observed, assuming the initial dose was insufficient?

## Correction
Adding extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] without improving [[Transfer|transfer]]. Effective learning design focuses on targeting the correct rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]] and avoiding mixing multiple skills in one unit. If a rule of thumb requires lengthy justification, the unit's target is likely wrong. The goal is to spend friction where it buys transfer, not to overload the learner.
