# Two-Speed Feedback

## When to use
This procedure is used when providing feedback within a learning unit, specifically within the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]]. It is applied after a learner has made a choice and provided a reason in a scenario.

## Steps
1.  **Provide the fast layer:** Offer a short [[Rule of Thumb]] the learner can remember and apply during a live conversation. This prevents [[Overthinking]].
2.  **Provide the slow layer:** Explain why the rule works and when it might fail. This prevents the formation of a [[Blind Habit]].

## Failure modes
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What happens if the rule of thumb is too simplistic? (2) How do you ensure the deeper explanation doesn't exceed the learner's [[Friction Budget]]?
