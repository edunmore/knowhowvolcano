# Toolbox Works Best for Observable Skills

## Rule
The [[Toolbox]] of learning techniques described works best when the target skill can be observed, even roughly. It can fail when outcomes are invisible or when the environment blocks practice (e.g., no permission to try, high risk, or strict scripts).

## Rationale
The methods rely on [[Calibration Loop|calibration]], [[Two-Speed Feedback]], and practice under constraints. These require the learner to make an observable choice and receive feedback on it. If the skill's outcomes are invisible or practice is blocked by the environment (like high risk or scripts), the core mechanisms of comparison and adjustment cannot function.
