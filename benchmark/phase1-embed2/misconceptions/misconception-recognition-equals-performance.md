# Recognition Equals Performance

## Misconception
The belief that if a learner can recognize or understand a concept in a learning context, they will be able to successfully perform the related skill in a real-world situation. This assumes understanding guarantees application.

## Correction
Recognition is distinct from performance. A learner may agree with a model but still fail to apply it under real-world pressures. True performance, and especially [[Transfer|transfer]] to new contexts under stress, requires deliberate practice spaced over time and practice under realistic constraints.
