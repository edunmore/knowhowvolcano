# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The mistaken belief that adding more examples and theoretical explanations to a learning unit inherently increases learning. This assumes that greater volume of content directly translates to better outcomes.

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise cognitive [[Friction Budget]] without improving [[Transfer (Level D)|transfer]]. Effective design prioritizes the essential rule of thumb and practice needed to reach the target [[Outcome Ladder]] level, rather than adding justifications or tangential information.
