# Bridge: Outcome Ladder ↔ 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

The Outcome Ladder and the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop are complementary frameworks for designing effective, goal-oriented learning. The Ladder provides the **structure** by defining a clear, ascending path from a simple action to a transformational goal. The Loop provides the **execution engine**, offering a repeatable, time-boxed procedure for climbing each rung.

Use them together when you need to break down a complex skill or behavioral change into tiny, actionable, and sustainable learning sprints. First, map the journey on the Outcome Ladder. Then, for the immediate next rung, employ the 7-Minute Loop: teach a micro-concept, engage the learner in a quick activity, and deliver immediate feedback—all focused on that specific rung’s outcome.

The key insight is that transformational goals (the top of the Ladder) are achieved not through willpower alone, but through a series of engineered, successful micro-experiences (the repeated Loop). The Ladder ensures each loop has strategic direction, while the Loop provides the tactical, frictionless method to make consistent progress, turning overwhelming change into manageable daily habits.
