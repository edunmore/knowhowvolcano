# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The wrong belief is that adding more examples and more theory to a learning unit inherently increases how much is learned. Teams often hold this belief.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What assumptions about knowledge transfer lead to this belief? (2) Is it driven by a desire for comprehensiveness or learner satisfaction?

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] without improving [[Transfer (Outcome Ladder)|transfer]]. Effective learning design should spend friction where it buys transfer, not on superfluous content. If a rule of thumb requires three paragraphs of justification, the unit may be targeting the wrong rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]] or mixing multiple skills.
