# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
When giving feedback within a [[Microlearning Loop]], deliver it in two layers: a fast, memorable rule of thumb for use in live situations, followed by a slow, deeper explanation of the underlying principle and its boundary conditions.

## Rationale
The fast layer prevents [[Overthinking]] and provides an accessible cue for immediate application, such as during a live conversation. The slow layer prevents [[Blind Habit]] by explaining why the rule works and when it might fail, fostering deeper understanding and appropriate transfer. This structure aligns with managing the learner's [[Friction Budget]] by separating immediate application from conceptual exploration.
