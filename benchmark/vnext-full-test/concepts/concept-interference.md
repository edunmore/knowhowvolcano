# Interference

## Definition
In the context of [[Microlearning]] design, interference is a root cause of performance failure where a learner, despite having knowledge, is prevented from applying it effectively in a real situation. It manifests as freezing under pressure, forgetting initial steps, or misapplying a tool due to a misunderstanding of its purpose.

## Operationalization
Interference is observed when learners fail to perform a target behavior in a realistic scenario despite prior exposure to the learning material. Specific indicators include: a person freezing in a live conversation, forgetting the first step of a procedure, or choosing an incorrect action because they misunderstood what a tool or technique is for.
