# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
When giving feedback in a learning context, it should be delivered in two layers: a fast, memorable [[Rule of Thumb]] the learner can use in live situations, followed by a slower, deeper explanation of why the rule works and its limitations.

## Rationale
The fast layer prevents [[Overthinking]] during performance, allowing for quick application. The slow layer prevents [[Blind Habit]] by building understanding, which aids in [[Transfer (Learning)]] to different contexts. This structure is a step within the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]] designed to optimize learning efficiency and combat [[Interference (Learning)]] under pressure.
