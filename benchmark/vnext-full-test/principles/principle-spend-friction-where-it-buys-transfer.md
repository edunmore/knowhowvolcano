# Spend Friction Where It Buys Transfer

## Rule
When designing a [[Microlearning]] unit, do not attempt to eliminate all friction. Instead, strategically allocate the learner's limited [[Friction Budget]] to mental effort that directly contributes to achieving [[Transfer (Learning)]] to real-world performance.

## Rationale
Every learner has a limited capacity for mental effort before they disengage. The principle recognizes that some friction is necessary for deep learning, but it must be spent wisely. The goal is to avoid wasting cognitive load on activities that do not move the learner up the [[Outcome Ladder]] (e.g., recalling terms or absorbing extra theory) and instead focus it on deliberate practice under [[Constraint (Learning Scenario)]]s that build the ability to perform under stress in varied contexts.
