# The 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

## When to use
Use this loop when designing a single microlearning unit to turn internal know-how into learning that sticks, especially when the goal is to move beyond superficial recall to real-world performance. It is presented as a toolbox to prevent [[Interference]] in performance under pressure.

## Steps
The procedure consists of seven steps for designing one unit:
1.  **Choose one [[Outcome Ladder]] rung** as the target (A, B, C, or D).
2.  **Write one "observable pass condition"**—what can be seen or measured.
3.  **Create one short scenario with a constraint** (e.g., time pressure, an annoyed colleague, missing data).
4.  **Offer two actions**: the intended action and a tempting wrong action.
5.  **Ask for a choice *and* a one-sentence reason.**
6.  **Give feedback in two layers**: first a short [[Rule of Thumb]], then a deeper explanation.
7.  **Add a "next repetition" prompt**: specify when and how to repeat (e.g., tomorrow, next meeting, next real case).

## Failure modes
