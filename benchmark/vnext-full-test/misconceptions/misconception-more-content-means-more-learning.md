# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples and more theoretical content to a learning unit inherently increases learning and improves [[Transfer (Learning)]] to real-world situations.

## Why it happens
The source text does not explicitly state the causes of this misconception. Open questions: (1) Is it driven by a desire to be comprehensive or authoritative? (2) Is it a confusion between information provision and skill development?

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] without improving transfer. Effective design focuses on spending the learner's limited mental effort where it buys transfer, such as through deliberate practice in the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]]. If a rule of thumb requires extensive justification, the unit may be targeting the wrong rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]] or mixing multiple skills.
