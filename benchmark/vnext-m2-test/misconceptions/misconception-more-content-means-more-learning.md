# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples and more [[Theory]] to a learning unit increases [[Learning]] and [[Transfer]].

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Why do teams default to adding content rather than improving practice? (2) Is this belief driven by an overemphasis on [[Knowledge Transfer (Rung A)]]?

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] without improving [[Transfer]]. Learning design should focus on spending [[Friction Budget|friction]] where it buys [[Transfer]], such as in deliberate practice within the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]]. If you need three paragraphs to justify a [[Rule of Thumb]], your unit is probably targeting the wrong [[Outcome Ladder|rung]] or mixing multiple [[Skills]].
