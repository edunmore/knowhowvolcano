# Friction Budget

## Definition
The **Friction Budget** is the maximum amount of mental effort a learner can expend on a learning unit before they disengage or resort to superficial strategies like guessing. It represents a capacity that can be exhausted by demanding too many new terms, steps, or cognitive switches at once.

## Operationalization
It is observed indirectly when learners "stop doing [[Deliberate Practice]] and default to superficial reading." Friction increases, and the budget is exceeded, when a learning unit requires "too many new terms at once, too many steps, or too much switching between screens." Designers must estimate this budget by analyzing the complexity of the unit's content and structure.
