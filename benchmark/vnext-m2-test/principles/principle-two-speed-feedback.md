# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
Feedback should be structured in two layers: a fast, memorable [[Rule of Thumb]] for use during a live conversation, followed by a slow, deeper explanation of why the rule works and when it fails.

## Rationale
This approach prevents both overthinking and blind habit formation. The fast layer provides immediate, actionable guidance under pressure, while the slow layer builds deeper [[Conceptual Understanding]] for future [[Transfer]].
