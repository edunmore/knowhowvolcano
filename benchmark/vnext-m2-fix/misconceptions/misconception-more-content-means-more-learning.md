# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples, more theory, or more explanatory content to a learning unit inherently increases the amount or quality of [[Learning|learning]].

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Is this driven by an assumption that coverage equals depth? (2) Does it stem from a desire to be comprehensive or to justify a rule?

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] without improving [[Transfer (Learning)|transfer]]. If a rule of thumb requires multiple paragraphs to justify, the unit is likely targeting the wrong rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]] or mixing multiple skills. Effective learning design spends the learner's limited friction budget where it directly buys transfer, not on superficial content volume.
