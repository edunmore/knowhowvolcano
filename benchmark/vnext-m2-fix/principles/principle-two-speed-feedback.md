# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
Feedback should be delivered in two layers: a fast, memorable [[Rule of Thumb]] and a slow, explanatory layer.

## Rationale
The fast layer prevents [[Overthinking]] during live situations by giving the learner a simple heuristic they can recall. The slow layer prevents [[Blind Habit]] by explaining the underlying reason the rule works and its boundary conditions. This structure is part of the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]] for designing effective learning units.
