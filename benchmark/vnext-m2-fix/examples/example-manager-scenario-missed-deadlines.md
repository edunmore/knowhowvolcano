# Manager Scenario: Missed Deadlines

## Scenario
A manager faces a team member who says, "I keep missing deadlines because other teams block me." The manager must choose between two specific actions to address this performance issue. This scenario is presented as a sample for a [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]].

## Context
The scenario is part of a design effort to turn internal know-how into short learning units for new managers. The broader context is a team producing "nice-to-read" pages that fail to prepare managers for real conversations under pressure, where [[Interference]] causes them to freeze, forget steps, or choose wrong tools. The scenario is intended to be a constrained practice element within a [[Microlearning]] unit.

## Key Insight
This example illustrates the application of the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]], specifically how to create a short, constrained scenario to practice managerial decision-making. It targets performance ([[Outcome Ladder]] Rung C) or transfer (Rung D) by forcing a choice under realistic pressure, moving beyond simple recognition or recall.

## Structure
- **Setup:** A team member reports a recurring problem of missed deadlines, blaming other teams.
- **Tension:** The manager must decide how to respond effectively in the moment.
- **Resolution:** The source text cuts off, but the implied structure is that the manager must choose between two provided actions (Action A: "Ask for a concrete recent example and...").
- **Outcome:** The scenario is designed for [[Calibration Loop|calibration]], where the learner commits to a decision and receives [[Two-Speed Feedback]] to understand the gap between their choice and an expert choice.
