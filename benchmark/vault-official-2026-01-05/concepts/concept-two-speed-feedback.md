# Two-Speed Feedback

## Definition
A feedback structure consisting of two distinct layers: a fast layer providing a memorable rule of thumb for use during live action, and a slow layer explaining the rule's rationale and limitations. This dual-speed design aims to balance immediate application with deeper understanding.

## Operationalization
Feedback is delivered in two parts. First, a short, actionable rule of thumb is given that a learner can recall in a live conversation. Second, a deeper explanation follows that clarifies why the rule works and identifies situations where it might fail.

## Boundary conditions
The source states the purpose of each layer: the fast layer prevents overthinking, and the slow layer prevents blind habit. The source also implies this feedback structure is a component within the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop, which works best when the skill can be observed.
