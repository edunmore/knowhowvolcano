# Calibration Loop

## Definition
A process where a learner improves their judgment by comparing their own choice to an expert's choice and understanding the difference between them. Calibration is accelerated when the learner must commit to a decision before seeing the answer and when the feedback explicitly identifies a hidden variable (e.g., risk, trust, time, power, or uncertainty).

## Operationalization
The concept is operationalized as a step within a learning scenario. A learner is presented with a choice (e.g., between Action A and Action B in a management scenario) and must commit to a decision and a reason. After committing, they compare their choice to an expert's choice. The feedback they receive must name the critical hidden variable that explains the expert's reasoning.
