# Recognition Is Performance

## Misconception
The mistaken belief that if a learner understands or agrees with a correct model or principle once, they will be able to apply it successfully in a real performance situation later.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What common training or feedback practices reinforce this belief? (2) Is this confusion more likely with certain types of knowledge or skills?

## Correction
Recognition is not performance. True transfer of learning requires repeated, spaced practice under realistic constraints (like time pressure or stress), not just a single moment of understanding. People can nod in agreement and still fail to perform the skill when needed.
