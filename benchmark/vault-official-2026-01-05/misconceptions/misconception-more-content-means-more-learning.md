# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples and more theoretical content to a learning unit will increase the amount of learning that occurs.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Does this belief stem from equating information density with instructional quality? (2) Is it a default approach when learning objectives are not clearly defined?

## Correction
Adding extra content can raise friction without improving transfer. If a rule of thumb requires extensive justification (e.g., three paragraphs), the learning unit is likely targeting the wrong rung of the Outcome Ladder or mixing multiple skills. The focus should be on designing for transfer within the learner's friction budget, not on content volume.
