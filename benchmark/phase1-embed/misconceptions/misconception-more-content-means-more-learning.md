# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples, theory, or content to a learning unit will automatically increase learning and improve performance.

## Correction
Adding extra content can raise [[cognitive load]] (or "friction") without improving [[transfer of learning]]. Effective learning design focuses on targeting the correct performance level (e.g., a specific rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]]) and spending the learner's limited mental effort where it directly supports skill application.
