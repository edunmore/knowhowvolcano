# Outcome Ladder

## Definition
The Outcome Ladder is a framework for separating learning outcomes into four distinct levels of capability, from basic recall to complex application. It helps clarify what "success looks like in the real world" by defining rungs for remembering terms, recognizing correct options, performing behaviors in realistic scenarios, and transferring skills to new contexts under stress.

## Operationalization
The ladder is operationalized through its four rungs: Rung A (remember/recall terms), Rung B (recognize/pick the right option from examples), Rung C (perform/do the behavior in a realistic scenario), and Rung D (transfer/do it later in a different context under stress). It is used as a target-setting tool in the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop, where a designer chooses one rung as the goal and writes an observable pass condition.
