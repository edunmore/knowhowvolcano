# Two-Speed Feedback

## Definition
A method of providing instructional feedback in two distinct layers. The first, fast layer is a simple, memorable rule of thumb a learner can recall during a live situation. The second, slow layer provides a deeper explanation of why the rule works and the conditions under which it might fail.

## Operationalization
Two-speed feedback is applied as part of the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop, specifically in Step 6. After a learner makes a choice and provides a reason, feedback is given: first, a short rule of thumb, and then a more detailed explanation.

## Boundary conditions
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What types of skills or topics is this feedback method most and least effective for? (2) How is the "slow layer" explanation best structured to avoid exceeding the learner's friction budget?
