# Recognition Equals Performance

## Misconception
The wrong belief is that if a person understands or agrees with a concept in a learning context, they will be able to perform the associated skill later when needed. This is summarized by the phrase "If they understood it once, they will perform it later."

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What common design or evaluation practices reinforce this belief? (2) What cognitive biases might lead educators or learners to conflate comprehension with capability?

## Correction
Recognition is not performance. The correction is that achieving real-world performance (transfer) requires deliberate practice under constraints and repetition spaced over time. People can understand a model but still fail to apply it under stress.
