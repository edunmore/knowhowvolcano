# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
Teams often believe that adding more examples and more theory increases learning.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What specific motivations lead teams to favor adding content? (2) Is this belief tied to assumptions about learner engagement or knowledge completeness?

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise friction without improving transfer. If you need three paragraphs to justify a rule of thumb, your unit is probably targeting the wrong rung of the Outcome Ladder or mixing multiple skills.
