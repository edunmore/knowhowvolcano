# Bridge: Outcome Ladder ↔ 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

The Outcome Ladder and the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop are complementary frameworks for designing effective learning. The Ladder is a **planning tool** that maps the incremental steps (Awareness, Understanding, Ability, Habit) from a learner's current state to a desired behavioral outcome. The 7-Minute Loop is an **execution tool** that structures a single, hyper-focused learning session (Explain, Demo, Do, Review).

They connect as **strategy and tactic**. Use the Outcome Ladder first to define the specific rung a learner needs to reach (e.g., "Ability to perform a task"). Then, use the 7-Minute Loop to build a session targeting that precise rung. For instance, a Loop could be designed to move a learner from "Understanding" a concept to the "Ability" to apply it through its "Do" phase.

The key insight is that microlearning is most powerful when it's not just a random chunk of content, but a **deliberate step on a progression**. The Ladder ensures each Loop has a clear destination within a larger journey, transforming isolated tactics into a cohesive learning strategy.
