# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples and more theoretical explanation to a learning unit inherently increases the amount a learner will learn or their ability to apply the knowledge.

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise [[friction-budget]] without improving [[learning-transfer]]. Excessive content can cause learners to exceed their mental effort budget, leading them to quit or engage in superficial reading rather than deliberate practice. Effective design focuses on the targeted learning outcome and spends the learner's effort where it directly contributes to skill transfer.
