# Recognition Equals Performance

## Misconception
The incorrect belief that if a learner can recognize or understand a concept in a learning context (e.g., by nodding at a good model), they will be able to perform the associated skill later in a real-world situation.

## Correction
Recognition is not performance. True skill transfer to real-world application requires spaced repetition over time and practice under realistic constraints or pressure, not just initial comprehension. This aligns with the higher levels of the [[Outcome Ladder]], such as Level 3 (author's 'Rung C': perform) and Level 4 (author's 'Rung D': transfer).
