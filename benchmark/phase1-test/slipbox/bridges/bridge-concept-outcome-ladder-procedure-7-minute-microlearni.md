# Bridge: Outcome Ladder ↔ 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

The Outcome Ladder and 7-Minute Microlearning Loop are a powerful pairing for designing focused, behavior-changing learning. The Outcome Ladder provides the *structure* by breaking down a large goal into a hierarchy of specific, observable actions. The 7-Minute Loop provides the *delivery method* for practicing and reinforcing one rung of that ladder at a time.

Use them together when you need to build a complex skill or habit incrementally. First, use the Outcome Ladder to map the progression from a simple starting action to the ultimate performance goal. Then, design a series of 7-minute microlearning sessions, each dedicated to explaining, demonstrating, and prompting practice for a single "rung" on that ladder.

The key insight is that this combination ensures microlearning has lasting impact beyond isolated knowledge. Instead of a disconnected 7-minute lesson, each loop becomes a deliberate step on a clear path to mastery, transforming brief engagements into a cohesive journey of behavioral change. The ladder gives direction; the loop provides the actionable step.
