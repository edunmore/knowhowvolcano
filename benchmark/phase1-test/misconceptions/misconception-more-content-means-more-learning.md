# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples and more theory to a [[Learning Unit]] will automatically increase learning. This is often held by teams designing training materials.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Is this a common assumption about learner motivation or engagement? (2) Does it stem from conflating coverage with comprehension?

## Correction
Adding extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|Friction]] without improving [[Transfer]]. Effective design targets a specific rung on the [[Outcome Ladder]] with a clear [[Observable Pass Condition]]. If a rule of thumb requires lengthy justification, the unit may be targeting the wrong rung or mixing multiple skills, exceeding the learner's capacity for [[Deliberate Practice]].
