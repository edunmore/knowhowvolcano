# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
Feedback should be delivered in two layers: a fast layer consisting of a memorable [[Rule of Thumb]], and a slow layer providing a deeper explanation of why the rule works and when it fails.

## Rationale
This structure addresses two distinct cognitive needs during [[Calibration Loop|calibration]]. The fast layer prevents [[Overthinking]] during live performance (e.g., a conversation), allowing the learner to apply a simple heuristic. The slow layer prevents [[Blind Habit]] formation by building a deeper, contextual understanding, which is necessary for [[Transfer]] to different contexts or under stress. This approach respects the [[Friction Budget]] by not overloading the learner with complex theory during the initial feedback moment.
