# Spend Friction Where It Buys Transfer

## Rule
Do not simply minimize friction in learning design; intentionally allocate a learner's limited [[Friction Budget]] to activities that directly promote [[Transfer (Outcome Ladder)|transfer]] (Rung D), such as practicing under realistic constraints, rather than wasting it on superficial content.

## Rationale
Every learning unit has a finite [[Friction Budget]] before learners quit or guess. Adding extra content or complexity raises friction without necessarily improving the ability to perform later under stress. The goal is to ensure the mental effort spent directly builds the learner's capacity to apply the skill in a different context, which is the hallmark of [[Transfer (Outcome Ladder)|transfer]]. This is achieved by designing for the [[Outcome Ladder]], using the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]] with constraints, and providing [[Two-Speed Feedback]] for calibration.
