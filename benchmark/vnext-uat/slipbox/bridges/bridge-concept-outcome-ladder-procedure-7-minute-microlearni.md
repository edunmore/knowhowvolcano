# Bridge: Outcome Ladder ↔ 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

The Outcome Ladder and the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop are a powerful strategic framework paired with an executional tactic. The Ladder provides the *what* and *why*—it defines a clear, ascending path from a current undesirable state to a desired future outcome through specific behavioral steps. The 7-Minute Loop provides the *how*—it’s a compact, repeatable procedure for learning or practicing one of those rungs on the ladder.

Use them together when you need to break down a complex skill or behavioral change into ultra-manageable, daily learning actions. The Ladder identifies which specific micro-skill to work on next, and the Loop provides the structured format to practice it in a focused burst.

The key insight is that sustainable progress is built by anchoring brief, tactical learning sessions (the Loop) to a strategic progression of outcomes (the Ladder). This prevents microlearning from becoming scattered and ensures each short session deliberately climbs one rung toward a larger goal.
