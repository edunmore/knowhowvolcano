# Calibration Loop

## Definition
A [[Calibration Loop]] is a learning process where a learner improves their judgment by explicitly comparing their own decision to an expert's decision and understanding the underlying reason for the difference. The goal is to refine the learner's mental model to better align with expert reasoning.

## Operationalization
Calibration is operationalized within a learning unit, such as the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]]. It occurs when a learner must first commit to a choice (e.g., between two actions in a scenario) before seeing the correct answer. Effective feedback then names the "hidden variable" (e.g., risk, trust, time, power, or uncertainty) that explains the difference between the learner's choice and the expert choice.
