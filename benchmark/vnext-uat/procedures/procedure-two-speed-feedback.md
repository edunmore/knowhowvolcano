# Two-Speed Feedback

## When to use
Use this procedure when delivering feedback within a [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]], particularly after a learner makes a choice in a practice scenario. It is designed to balance immediate usability with deeper understanding.

## Steps
1.  **Provide a fast layer of feedback.** This is a short [[Rule of Thumb]] the learner can remember and use during a live conversation.
2.  **Provide a slow layer of feedback.** This explains *why* the rule works and *when* it might fail.
3.  **Sequence the feedback.** Deliver the fast layer first to prevent [[Overthinking]], then the slow layer to prevent [[Blind Habit]].

## Failure modes
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What happens if the fast layer rule is too simplistic for the context? (2) How do you ensure the slow layer explanation doesn't itself exceed the learner's [[Friction Budget]]?
