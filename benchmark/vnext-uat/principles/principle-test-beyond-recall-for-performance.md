# Test Beyond Recall For Performance

## Rule
When designing learning, the primary test of success should target the [[Outcome Ladder]] rung of performance (Rung C) or transfer (Rung D), not just recall (Rung A) or recognition (Rung B).

## Rationale
Testing only for recall leads to a false sense of progress while real-world performance remains flat. People can recall terms or nod at a model but still fail to perform the behavior under realistic [[Scenario|scenarios]] or pressure. This principle addresses the real problem of *interference*, where learners freeze, forget steps, or choose the wrong tool in a live situation. Focusing assessments on performance ensures that learning units are designed to build skills that stick and transfer.
