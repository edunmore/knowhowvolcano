# Bridge: Outcome Ladder ↔ 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

The Outcome Ladder and the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop are a powerful pair for structured skill development. The Ladder provides the *what* and *why*—it’s a hierarchy of goals from a basic task to a meaningful life impact. The Loop provides the *how*—a repeatable, time-boxed procedure for practicing and internalizing a single step.

Use them together when you need to break down a complex skill into learnable, actionable chunks. First, map your desired skill onto the Outcome Ladder to identify the specific, granular rung you want to work on now (e.g., "I can write a clear prompt for an AI image generator"). Then, use the 7-Minute Loop to practice just that: spend minutes 1-2 learning the prompt formula, minutes 3-5 writing three practice prompts, and minutes 6-7 reviewing and correcting them.

The key insight is that the Ladder ensures your micro-efforts are aligned with a larger purpose, while the Loop provides a frictionless engine for consistent, incremental progress up each rung. This turns abstract ambition into a series of manageable, seven-minute sessions.
