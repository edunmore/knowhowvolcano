# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The mistaken belief that adding more examples and more theoretical explanation to a learning unit inherently increases the amount or quality of learning that occurs.

## Correction
Adding extra content can increase cognitive friction without improving the transfer of skills to real-world contexts. Effective learning design focuses on achieving a specific performance outcome (e.g., a target level on the [[Outcome Ladder]]) with minimal, purposeful content. If extensive justification is needed for a core idea, the unit may be targeting the wrong skill level or conflating multiple skills.
