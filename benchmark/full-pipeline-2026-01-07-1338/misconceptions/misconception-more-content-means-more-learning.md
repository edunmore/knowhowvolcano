# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The wrong belief is that adding more examples and more theory to a learning unit will increase the amount of learning.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What psychological or organizational factors lead teams to adopt this belief? (2) Is this belief more common in certain types of learning design?

## Correction
The correction is that extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] without improving [[Transfer (Learning)]]. If a unit requires extensive justification for a [[Rule Of Thumb|rule of thumb]], it may be targeting the wrong rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]] or mixing multiple skills. Learning design should spend friction where it buys transfer.
