# Outcome Ladder

## Definition
The [[Outcome Ladder]] is a framework for separating learning outcomes into four distinct rungs of increasing complexity. The rungs progress from basic recall to the ability to apply a skill in a different context under stress. It is used to clarify the target of a learning design by asking what real-world success looks like for a learner.

## Operationalization
The ladder is used by choosing one of its four rungs as a target when designing a learning unit. The rungs are defined as: **Rung A: remember** (can recall terms), **Rung B: recognize** (can pick the right option from examples), **Rung C: perform** (can do the behavior in a realistic scenario), and **Rung D: transfer** (can do it later, in a different context, under stress). An [[Observable Pass Condition]] is then written to measure success at the chosen rung.
