# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
Feedback should be delivered in two layers: a fast, memorable "rule of thumb" for use during live action, followed by a slower, deeper explanation of why the rule works and its limitations.

## Rationale
This two-layer structure serves distinct purposes. The fast layer "prevents overthinking" by providing a simple heuristic a learner can recall and apply in the moment, such as during a live conversation. The slow layer "prevents blind habit" by building understanding of the underlying principles, conditions for success, and potential failure modes, which supports [[Transfer]] to different contexts.
