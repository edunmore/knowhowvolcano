# Bridge: Outcome Ladder ↔ 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

The Outcome Ladder and the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop are complementary frameworks for structured learning. The Ladder is a conceptual map, defining a hierarchy of goals from simple tasks to complex outcomes. The Loop is an execution engine, providing a repeatable procedure for brief, focused study sessions.

They relate as strategy and tactic. You use the Outcome Ladder first to analyze and sequence a complex skill into ascending rungs (e.g., from "identify a chord" to "improvise a solo"). Then, you apply the 7-Minute Loop to actively practice a single, specific rung. Each micro-session (Learn, Recall, Apply, Reflect) tackles one small step on the ladder, enabling steady, incremental ascent.

The key insight is that this pairing prevents microlearning from becoming fragmented. The Loop ensures actionable practice, while the Ladder provides the crucial narrative context, ensuring each 7-minute investment contributes to a meaningful, larger competency. Together, they turn overwhelming goals into a sustainable cycle of micro-progress.
