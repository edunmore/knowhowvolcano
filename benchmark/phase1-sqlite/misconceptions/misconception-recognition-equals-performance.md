# Recognition Equals Performance

## Misconception
The wrong belief that if learners can recognize or understand a concept in a learning context, they will be able to perform the corresponding skill in a real-world, high-stakes situation. The author states this as: "If they understood it once, they will perform it later."

## Correction
Recognition is not performance. True performance, especially the ability to transfer a skill to new contexts under stress, requires deliberate practice spaced over time and practice under realistic constraints. This is a distinct, higher level of competence (Stage: Perform/Transfer) compared to the lower stage of merely recognizing correct answers (Stage: Recognize).
