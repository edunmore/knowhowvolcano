# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples, explanations, or theoretical content to a learning unit will inherently lead to more or better learning outcomes.

## Correction
Adding extra content often increases cognitive friction without improving real-world transfer of skills. Effective design focuses on a clear target outcome (e.g., a specific rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]]) and spends the learner's limited "friction budget" deliberately on activities that directly promote transfer, not on superfluous information. If a rule of thumb requires lengthy justification, the unit may be targeting the wrong skill level or mixing multiple skills.
