# Calibration Loop

## Definition
A Calibration Loop is a learning process where a learner improves their judgment by comparing their own decision to an expert's decision and understanding the difference. This comparison is most effective when the learner commits to a choice before seeing the answer.

## Key Components
1.  **Commitment to a Decision:** The learner must make and commit to a specific choice before receiving feedback.
2.  **Expert Comparison:** The learner's choice is compared against an expert's choice or model answer.
3.  **Diagnostic Feedback:** The feedback explicitly identifies the underlying factor or "hidden variable" (e.g., risk, trust, time, power, or uncertainty) that explains the difference between the choices.

## Application
The loop is a core mechanism within the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]], specifically in the feedback step. It is used to accelerate the development of accurate judgment for skills that can be observed, by focusing feedback on the critical variable that separates novice and expert performance.
