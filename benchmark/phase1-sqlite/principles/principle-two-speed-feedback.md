# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
Feedback should be delivered in two layers: a fast, memorable rule of thumb for immediate application, followed by a slower, deeper explanation of the underlying rationale and its limits.

## Rationale
This principle balances cognitive load to aid [[Transfer (Stage 4)|transfer]]. The fast layer prevents overthinking in live situations, while the slow layer prevents blind habit by explaining "why the rule works and when it fails." This approach respects the [[Friction Budget]] by avoiding excessive content that raises effort without improving performance.
