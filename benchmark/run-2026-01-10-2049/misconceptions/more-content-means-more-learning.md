# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples, theory, or content to a learning unit inherently increases learning.

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] (mental effort) without improving [[Outcome Ladder|transfer]] of skills to real-world performance. Effective learning design focuses on spending the learner's limited friction budget on activities that directly build toward the target outcome level, rather than on superfluous information.
