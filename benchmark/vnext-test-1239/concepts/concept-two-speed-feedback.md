# Two-Speed Feedback

## Definition
A [[Feedback]] structure for learning that delivers information in two distinct layers. The fast layer provides a memorable rule of thumb for use during live situations, while the slow layer offers a deeper explanation of why the rule works and when it might fail. This design prevents overthinking in the moment while also preventing the formation of blind habits.

## Operationalization
It is applied within a structured learning unit, such as the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]]. After a learner makes a choice and provides a reason, feedback is given in two parts: first the short rule, then the deeper explanation. The intended effect is observable when learners can recall and apply the fast rule under pressure and later understand its rationale.
