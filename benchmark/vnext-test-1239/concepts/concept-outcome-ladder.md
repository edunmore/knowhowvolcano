# Outcome Ladder

## Definition
The **Outcome Ladder** is a framework for classifying the intended results of a learning unit into four distinct levels or "rungs." It separates outcomes from simple recall to complex application, helping designers target specific performance levels. The rungs are: A) Remember, B) Recognize, C) Perform, and D) Transfer.

## Operationalization
The ladder is operationalized by identifying which rung a learning unit targets and writing an "observable pass condition" for that rung. For example, targeting Rung C (`perform`) requires a scenario where the learner can demonstrate the behavior, while Rung D (`transfer`) requires doing it later, in a different context, under stress. The choice of rung directly informs the design of the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]].

## Boundary conditions
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Is the ladder intended only for designing [[Microlearning]] units, or for any instructional design? (2) Are the four rungs a fixed, hierarchical sequence, or can they be targeted independently?
