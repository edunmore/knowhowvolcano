# Bridge: Outcome Ladder ↔ 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

The Outcome Ladder and the 7-Minute Microlearning Loop connect as a powerful goal-action pairing for skill development. The Ladder is a planning tool that breaks a large, complex skill into a sequence of smaller, observable outcomes. The Loop is an execution engine—a structured procedure for practicing one discrete rung of that ladder in a focused, seven-minute session.

Use them together when you need to systematically build a complex capability without overwhelming yourself. First, use the Outcome Ladder to map the progression from novice to competent, defining each clear step. Then, use the Microlearning Loop to repeatedly practice and master a single, current rung through its cycle of Learn, Recall, and Apply.

The key insight is that the Ladder provides the strategic "what" and "why," ensuring your micro-efforts are coherently building toward a larger goal. The Loop provides the tactical "how," offering a sustainable, brain-friendly method for achieving each small outcome. Together, they transform a daunting skill gap into a series of manageable, daily wins.
