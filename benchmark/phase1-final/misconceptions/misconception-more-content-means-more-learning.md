# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
A common belief in instructional design is that adding more examples, explanations, or theoretical content will inherently increase the depth or effectiveness of learning.

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise cognitive load and exceed the learner's [[Friction Budget]] without improving their ability to transfer the skill to real-world situations. Effective design focuses on a clear, single-skill target (using the [[Outcome Ladder]]) and spends "friction" only where it directly buys skill transfer.
