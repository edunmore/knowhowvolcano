# Recognition Equals Performance

## Misconception
The incorrect belief that a learner's ability to recognize or understand a concept during instruction (e.g., nodding at a good model) is equivalent to being able to successfully perform the associated skill in a real-world context, especially under pressure.

## Correction
Recognition is not performance. True performance, or [[transfer]], requires deliberate practice spaced over time and practice under realistic constraints (e.g., time pressure, stress). This aligns with the distinction between lower rungs (like Level 2: Recognize) and higher rungs (like Level 4: Transfer) on the [[Outcome Ladder]].
