# 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

## When to use
Use this procedure to design a single, short learning unit aimed at building a specific, observable skill, particularly when the goal is to overcome performance interference in real-world, stressful situations.

## Steps
1.  **Target a specific learning outcome (author's "Choose one Outcome Ladder rung").** Define the unit's goal as one level of performance: recall, recognition, performance in a scenario, or transfer to a new context.
2.  **Define an observable pass condition.** Specify what successful performance looks like in a measurable or visible way.
3.  **Create a constrained scenario.** Develop a short, realistic situation that includes a constraint like time pressure, an emotional stakeholder, or incomplete information.
4.  **Offer two distinct action choices.** Present one intended (correct) action and one tempting but incorrect alternative.
5.  **Require a choice and a rationale.** Ask the learner to select an action *and* provide a one-sentence reason for their choice.
6.  **Provide two-speed feedback.** Deliver immediate feedback with a fast, memorable rule of thumb, followed by a slower, deeper explanation of the underlying principle.
7.  **Schedule a repetition.** Add a prompt specifying when and how to practice the skill again (e.g., tomorrow, in the next meeting).
