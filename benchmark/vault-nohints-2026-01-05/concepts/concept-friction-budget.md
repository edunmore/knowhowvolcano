# Friction Budget

## Definition
The Friction Budget is the maximum amount of mental effort a learner can spend on a learning unit before they quit or start guessing. It is a design constraint for microlearning, where exceeding the budget causes learners to stop doing deliberate practice and default to superficial reading.

## Operationalization
Friction in a learning unit grows when it requires too many new terms at once, too many steps, or too much switching between screens. A designer can identify excessive friction by checking for these factors against the defined budget for mental effort.
