# Outcome Ladder

## Definition
The Outcome Ladder is a tool used to clarify the real-world goal of learning. It separates learning outcomes into four distinct levels or "rungs": A) remember, B) recognize, C) perform, and D) transfer.

## Operationalization
It is operationalized as a set of four distinct rungs. Rung A is the ability to recall terms. Rung B is the ability to pick the right option from examples. Rung C is the ability to do the behavior in a realistic scenario. Rung D is the ability to do it later, in a different context, under stress.
