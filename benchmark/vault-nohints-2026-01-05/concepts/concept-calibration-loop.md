# Calibration Loop

## Definition
A calibration loop is a learning process where a learner improves their judgment by comparing their own choice or decision to an expert choice and then understanding the difference between them.

## Operationalization
Calibration occurs fastest when two specific conditions are met: first, the learner must commit to a decision before seeing the answer, and second, the feedback they receive must explicitly name the "hidden variable" (such as risk, trust, time, power, or uncertainty) that explains the difference between their choice and the expert choice.

## Boundary conditions
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Does the calibration loop apply only to observable skills, or also to internal decision-making? (2) How does the loop function in environments that block practice, such as high-risk situations or strict scripts?
