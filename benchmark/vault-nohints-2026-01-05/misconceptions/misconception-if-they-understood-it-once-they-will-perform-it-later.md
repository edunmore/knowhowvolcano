# If They Understood It Once, They Will Perform It Later

## Misconception
The wrong belief is that once a learner understands a concept or model (i.e., can recognize and agree with it), they will be able to reliably perform or apply that skill later, especially in different contexts or under pressure.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What common experiences or teaching practices lead to this belief? (2) Are there specific organizational or cultural factors that reinforce equating comprehension with capability?

## Correction
Recognition is not performance. The ability to transfer a skill to a later, different, or stressful situation requires repetition spaced over time and practice under specific constraints.
