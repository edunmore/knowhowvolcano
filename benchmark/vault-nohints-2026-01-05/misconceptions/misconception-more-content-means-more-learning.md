# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples and more theoretical content to a learning unit inherently increases learning.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What specific desires or pressures lead teams to believe this? (2) Is this belief more common for certain types of skills or learning objectives?

## Correction
In reality, extra content can raise friction without improving transfer. Adding material can exceed the learner's friction budget, causing them to stop deliberate practice and default to superficial reading. If multiple paragraphs are needed to justify a rule of thumb, the unit may be targeting the wrong outcome rung or mixing multiple skills.
