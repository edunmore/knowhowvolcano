# Common Mistake of Testing Only Rung A

## Rule
A common mistake in designing learning is to test only for recall of terms (Rung A of the Outcome Ladder) and celebrate progress while actual performance on higher rungs remains flat.

## Rationale
Testing only the lowest rung (remembering) creates a false sense of progress because recognition is not performance. Real-world application requires the ability to perform a behavior in realistic scenarios (Rung C) and transfer it to new contexts under stress (Rung D), which necessitates practice under constraints and spaced repetition.
