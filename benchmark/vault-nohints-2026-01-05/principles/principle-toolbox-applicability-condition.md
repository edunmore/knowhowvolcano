# Toolbox Applicability Condition

## Rule
The learning toolbox (of techniques like Two-Speed Feedback and the Calibration Loop) works best when the skill being learned can be observed, even roughly. It can fail when outcomes are invisible or when the environment blocks practice.

## Rationale
For a learner to improve, they need to be able to see their performance and attempt the skill. The toolbox fails when practice is prevented by factors like having no permission to try, high risk, or strict scripts.
