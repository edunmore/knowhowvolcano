# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
Feedback should be delivered in two layers: a fast layer consisting of a memorable rule of thumb for use during a live conversation, and a slow layer that explains why the rule works and when it fails.

## Rationale
The fast layer prevents overthinking by providing a simple, actionable heuristic that can be recalled under pressure. The slow layer prevents blind habit by offering the deeper rationale and boundary conditions, fostering a more robust understanding. This dual structure addresses the interference that causes people to freeze or choose the wrong tool in real-time situations.
