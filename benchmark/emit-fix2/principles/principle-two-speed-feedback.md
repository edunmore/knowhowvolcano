# Two-Speed Feedback

## Rule
When giving feedback, provide it in two layers: a fast, memorable [[Rule of Thumb]] for use during a live conversation, followed by a slow, deeper explanation of why the rule works and when it might fail.

## Rationale
The two layers serve distinct [[Cognitive Load]] functions. The fast layer prevents [[Overthinking]] under pressure, allowing a learner to act. The slow layer prevents the formation of a [[Blind Habit]] by building [[Transfer|Transferable]] understanding of the underlying principles and boundary conditions.
