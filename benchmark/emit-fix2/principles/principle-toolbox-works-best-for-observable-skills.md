# Toolbox Works Best for Observable Skills

## Rule
The methods described in the chapter—including the [[Two-Speed Feedback]] model, the [[Calibration Loop]], and the diagnostic approach in the sample scenario—are most effective when the skill to be learned can be observed, even roughly.

## Rationale
The [[Friction Budget]] and learning techniques are designed for skills where learners can practice and receive feedback. The principle fails when outcomes are invisible or when the environment prevents practice, such as when there is no permission to try, high risk, or strict scripts. This highlights the importance of context and the limits of [[Transfer]] when practice is blocked.
