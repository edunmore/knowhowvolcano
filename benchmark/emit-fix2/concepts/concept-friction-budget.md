# Friction Budget

## Definition
The **Friction Budget** is the maximum amount of mental effort a [[Learner]] can spend on a [[Microlearning]] unit before they disengage, quit, or resort to superficial reading and guessing. It is a limited resource that is consumed when a learning unit presents too many new [[Term]]s, too many steps, or requires excessive switching between contexts.

## Operationalization
Friction can be observed when a learning unit requires "too many new terms at once, too many steps, or too much switching between screens." When the budget is exceeded, learners stop [[Deliberate Practice]] and default to superficial reading. The budget's limit is not explicitly measured quantitatively but is identified by these behavioral markers of disengagement.
