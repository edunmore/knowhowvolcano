# 7-Minute Microlearning Loop

## When to use
Use this loop to design one [[Microlearning]] unit, especially when the goal is to turn internal know-how into short, practical learning that transfers to real-world performance under pressure. It addresses the problem of *interference*, where learners freeze or forget steps in stressful situations.

## Steps
1.  **Choose one target rung** from the [[Outcome Ladder]] (A, B, C, or D).
2.  **Write one “observable pass condition”** (what can be seen or measured).
3.  **Create one short scenario** with a constraint (e.g., time pressure, an annoyed colleague, missing data).
4.  **Offer two actions**: the intended action and a tempting wrong action.
5.  **Ask for a choice *and* a one-sentence reason.**
6.  **Give [[Two-Speed Feedback]]**: first a short [[Rule Of Thumb]], then a deeper explanation.
7.  **Add a “next repetition” prompt**: specify when and how to repeat the practice (e.g., tomorrow, next meeting, next real case).

## Failure modes
