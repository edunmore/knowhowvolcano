# More Content Means More Learning

## Misconception
The belief that adding more examples, theory, or content to a learning unit will automatically increase the amount that is learned. Teams often hold this belief, but it is mistaken.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Why do teams default to adding more content? (2) Is this belief linked to conflating information delivery with skill development?

## Correction
Adding extra content can raise [[Friction Budget|friction]] without improving [[Transfer (Learning)|transfer]]. The goal is to spend friction where it buys transfer, not to add content for its own sake. If justifying a [[Rule of Thumb]] requires excessive explanation, the unit may be targeting the wrong rung of the [[Outcome Ladder]] or mixing multiple skills.
