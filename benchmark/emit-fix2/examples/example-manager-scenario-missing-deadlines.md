# Manager Scenario: Missing Deadlines

## Scenario
A manager is presented with a situation where a team member says, "I keep missing deadlines because other teams block me." The learner (the manager) must choose between two provided actions in response. This scenario is used as a sample within a [[Microlearning]] design framework.

## Context
The scenario is presented as a realistic, constrained situation for practice, designed according to the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]]. It is used to help new managers move beyond remembering concepts ([[Rung A: Remember]]) to performing behaviors ([[Rung C: Perform]]) under realistic pressure. The broader context is a team trying to turn internal know-how into effective learning units that prevent [[Interference]] during real conversations.

## Key Insight
This example illustrates the application of the [[7-Minute Microlearning Loop]] for designing practice that builds [[Transfer (Learning)]]. It demonstrates how to create a short, constrained scenario with a clear choice to help learners [[Calibration|calibrate]] their decision-making, moving them beyond simple recognition to performance.

## Structure
- **Setup:** A manager is confronted by a team member who is consistently missing deadlines and blames other teams.
- **Tension:** The manager must decide how to respond effectively in the moment.
- **Resolution:** The learner must commit to one of two provided actions before receiving feedback.
- **Outcome:** The scenario is designed to provide [[Two-Speed Feedback]] to improve the learner's future real-world performance.

## Rewrite Opportunity
- **Core Pattern:** The pattern is a constrained, binary-choice scenario focusing on a common managerial friction point (handling excuses/blame).
- **Transferable Elements:** The structure (problem statement + two actionable choices) can be applied to other domains like giving feedback, handling conflict, or prioritizing work. The key is to include a "tempting wrong action" that reflects a common [[Cognitive Bias|bias]] or mistake.
