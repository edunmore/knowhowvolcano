# Symbiotic Organization-People Relationship

## Rule
When an organization adopts a coaching style of leadership, it should position itself as a platform for people to achieve their potential, transforming the traditional relationship into a symbiotic one.

## Rationale
This works because the coaching style directly enables individual growth, which in turn feeds back into the organization's capabilities, creating a mutually reinforcing cycle where both the organization and its people thrive together.
