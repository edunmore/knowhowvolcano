# Coaching Focuses on Future Possibilities

## Rule
Coaching focuses on future possibilities, not past mistakes. The practice is oriented toward a journey of discovery and improving future performance, rather than instruction or dwelling on errors.

## Rationale
The source text frames coaching as a journey, where the coachee develops skills "by discovering from within, stimulated by coaching." This forward-looking approach is linked to the "Inner Game" concept, which posits that removing internal obstacles allows an "unexpected natural ability to learn and to perform" to emerge. Focusing on future possibilities facilitates this internal discovery and natural performance improvement.
