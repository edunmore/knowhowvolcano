# Inner Game Equation

## Rule
Performance is equal to potential minus interference. The objective of coaching is to improve performance by growing potential and decreasing interference.

## Rationale
The equation, P = p – i, effectively summarizes the objective of modern coaching. It is grounded in the insight that internal obstacles to performance are often more formidable than external ones. If a coach helps reduce these internal obstacles (interference), a person's natural ability to learn and perform can emerge without the need for extensive technical instruction from the coach. This principle shifts the focus from teaching to unlocking a person's inherent potential.
