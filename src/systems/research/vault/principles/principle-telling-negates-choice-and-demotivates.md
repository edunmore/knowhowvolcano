# Telling Negates Choice and Demotivates

## Rule
The leadership behavior of "telling" (or command-and-control) negates an individual's choice, disempowers them, limits their potential, and consequently demotivates them. In contrast, coaching does the opposite.

## Rationale
Self-belief is built when someone is seen as worthy of making choices. "Telling" directly removes this opportunity for choice, which is counterproductive to building self-belief. The source text contrasts this with the needs of modern employees, especially younger ones, who seek meaning, purpose, and the opportunity to express their potential—needs that are frustrated by a telling approach. Furthermore, the text identifies that factors like a perceived shortage of time and excess fear drive leaders toward command-and-control behavior, which sidelines developmental coaching.
