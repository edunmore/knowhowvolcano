# Standards and Quality are Key in Coaching

## Rule
The quality of coaching must be high, and maintaining high standards is a fundamental requirement.

## Rationale
The source text explicitly states that the quality of coaching taught is high and that "standards and quality are key." This emphasis on quality is reflected in the material, which uses workplace dialogues intended to reflect the coaching level required of a professional coaching certification.
