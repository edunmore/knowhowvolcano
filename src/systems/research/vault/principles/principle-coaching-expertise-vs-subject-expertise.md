# Coaching Expertise vs. Subject Expertise

## Rule
Coaching expertise is not dependent on the coach's subject-matter expertise; it is a distinct skill in the coaching process itself. A key discipline for a coaching leader is to learn when to share their own knowledge and experience and when not to.

## Rationale
Sharing knowledge as a more experienced person (as in mentoring) can undermine the building of self-belief, which creates sustained performance. Instead, effective coaching works from the coachee's own experience and perceptions, which allows them to progress beyond the coach's own knowledge and limitations. This independence from subject expertise is one of coaching's great strengths.
