# Coaching In Organizational Context

## Rule
Coaching must be taught and learned within the specific context of an organization, not as a generic skill.

## Rationale
This is crucial because the learners will be operating within organizational environments, and the core subject (*Coaching for Performance*) is designed for and defined by that application context. Learning in the target context ensures relevance and immediate applicability.
