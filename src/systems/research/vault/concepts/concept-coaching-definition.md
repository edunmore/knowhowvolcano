# Coaching Definition

## Definition
Coaching is unlocking people's potential to maximize their own performance. It is a process focused on future possibilities, centered on helping individuals learn and discover from within rather than being instructed or taught. The powerful working relationship and the means and style of communication are key to its effectiveness.

## Operationalization
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What specific observable actions or conversational patterns distinguish a coaching interaction from instruction? (2) How can the reduction of 'interference' or the growth of 'potential' be measured in practice?

## Boundary conditions
Coaching is explicitly contrasted with instruction, teaching, and the giving of technical input. It applies when the objective is to improve performance by fostering the coachee's own discovery and learning capability. The source implies it may not apply in contexts where the primary goal is direct knowledge transfer or authoritative instruction, as these disrupt natural learning.
