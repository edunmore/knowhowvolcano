# Coaching Style of Leadership

## Definition
A style of leadership practiced by people leaders and managers that focuses on unlocking potential to deliver the highest level of performance. It is presented as a new type of leadership befitting the twenty-first century, distinct from old habits that do not enable people to fulfill their potential.

## Operationalization
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) What specific observable behaviors differentiate a "leader coach" from a traditional manager? (2) How is the "highest level of performance" measured or recognized in this context?

## Boundary conditions
The source implies this style applies specifically within organizational contexts, where leaders aim to develop their teams. It is contrasted with "old habits" of leadership. The term "leader coach" is used to signify a practice distinct from traditional management.
