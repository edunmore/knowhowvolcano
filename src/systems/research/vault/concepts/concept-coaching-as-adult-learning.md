# Coaching as Adult Learning

## Definition
Coaching is defined as adult learning in practice. It is a mechanism for transformation that enables leaders to develop people by tapping into their potential, based on the principle that adults learn differently than children.

## Operationalization
Coaching is operationalized through a partnership and collaboration that believes in potential. It is applied using specific skills and a framework: the foundational skills of powerful questioning and active listening, guided by the GROW model as a compass. This practice aims to foster awareness and responsibility, which are central to coaching and high performance.

## Boundary conditions
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Does coaching as adult learning apply to all developmental stages of a leader, or only after they have evolved beyond self-interest? (2) Is it ineffective in environments dominated by traditional command-and-control management methods?
