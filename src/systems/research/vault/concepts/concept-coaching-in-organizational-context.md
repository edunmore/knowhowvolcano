# Coaching in the Context of an Organization

## Definition
Coaching in the context of an organization is the practice of applying coaching skills specifically within an organizational setting, whether as a formal coach (internal or external) or as a leader using a coaching style. It involves combining the core principles of coaching with the practical realities and objectives of a business to create a powerful experience for both the individual coachee and the organization.

## Boundary conditions
This context applies specifically to coaching delivered to people within organizations, including formal 1:1 sessions and leadership interactions. It does not apply to coaching in personal, non-organizational settings like life coaching for private individuals. The source also implies a boundary where specific skills may be solely applicable to either formal coaches or leader-coaches.
