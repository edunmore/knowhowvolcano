# Coaching In Organizational Context

## Definition
Coaching for performance is an activity that must be learned and practiced within the specific context of an organization, as that is the environment where the coaching will ultimately be applied.

## Why it matters
It is crucial because the learners will be operating within an organizational setting, and the effectiveness of coaching is tied to its relevance and application within that real-world context.
