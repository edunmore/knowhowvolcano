# Leader Coach

## Definition
A leader coach is a term developed by organizations and leaders to signify the practice of a distinct type of leadership that elevates their abilities to a new level.

## Why it matters
It is used to demonstrate a fundamental shift from conventional leadership or management approaches, positioning it as a more advanced and effective practice.
