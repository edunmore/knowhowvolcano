# Leader Coach

## Definition
A leader coach is a term developed by organizations and the leaders within them to signify the practice of a completely different type of leadership or management that elevates their abilities to a whole new level. It represents a shift towards a coaching style of leadership that aims to unlock potential and deliver high performance.

## Operationalization
A leader coach can be observed as a leader who is developing and applying a coaching style. This involves trying new behaviors and using tools and questions to unlock potential rather than simply directing, which may be a change from their previous style. Leaders may explicitly tell colleagues they are developing these new leadership skills to foster understanding and support for the behavioral change.
