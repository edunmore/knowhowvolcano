# Foundation of Coaching: Awareness and Responsibility

## Definition
The foundation of coaching is the principle that performance is maximized by unlocking a person's inherent potential through self-discovery, not instruction. This is achieved by focusing on future possibilities and helping the coachee remove internal obstacles (interference), which allows their natural learning capability to flourish.

## Operationalization
Coaching is observed as a powerful working relationship and a specific style of communication where the coach facilitates discovery. The coachee acquires facts and develops skills "by discovering from within, stimulated by coaching." The process can be summarized by the Inner Game Equation (P = p – i), where the coach's role is to help grow potential (p) and decrease interference (i) to improve performance (P).
