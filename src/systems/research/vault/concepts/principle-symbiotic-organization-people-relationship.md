# Symbiotic Organization-People Relationship

## Definition
A relationship between an organization and its people that is mutually beneficial and interdependent, where the organization serves as the platform for individuals to achieve their potential, and this achievement in turn benefits the organization.

## Why it matters
It represents the final, evolved state of the organization-people dynamic, enabled by the widespread adoption of a coaching style of leadership, moving beyond traditional transactional or hierarchical models.
