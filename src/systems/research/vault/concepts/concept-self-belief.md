# Self-Belief

## Definition
In the context of Maslow's hierarchy of needs, self-belief is a personal esteem need, distinguished from "status and recognition" from others. It is the belief in oneself, which, when satisfied alongside esteem from others, can lead to the self-actualizing state.

## Operationalization
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) How is an individual's level of self-belief assessed or observed in a work context? (2) What specific behaviors indicate the presence or absence of self-belief?

## Boundary conditions
The source distinguishes self-belief from external factors: it is not created by "prestige and privilege," which are symbolic. It is also counterproductive when attempted through actions like "promotion without genuine empowerment and the opportunity to express potential."
