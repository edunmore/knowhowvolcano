# Coachee

## Definition
The coachee is the person who is being coached, regardless of whether the coaching occurs in a peer, team member, leader, or formal coaching session context.

## Why it matters
Using a single, simplified term ("coachee") for the recipient of coaching across different relational contexts provides clarity and consistency in discussing coaching dynamics.
