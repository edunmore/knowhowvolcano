# Coaching Is Teaching

## Misconception
The wrong belief is that coaching is synonymous with instruction, training, or private tuition. This view defines coaching as a process where a coach tells or teaches a coachee facts, skills, and behaviors.

## Why it happens
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Why do popular dictionary definitions of "coaching" emphasize instruction and teaching? (2) What factors lead people to rely on these common definitions over the professional meaning?

## Correction
Coaching is not about instruction or teaching. It is a future-focused process centered on a journey, where the coachee discovers facts and develops skills from within, stimulated by the coaching relationship and communication. The objective is to improve performance by growing potential and decreasing internal interference, not by providing technical input.
