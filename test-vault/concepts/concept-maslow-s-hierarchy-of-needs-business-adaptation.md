# Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs (Business Adaptation)

## Definition
A psychological model, adapted for a business and coaching context, which suggests human needs are arranged in a hierarchy from basic physiological needs to higher psychological needs. It is presented as a framework for understanding motivation and is linked to organizational culture and workplace motivators.

## Operationalization
The model is observed as a six-layer pyramid. Each horizontal layer contains a core need (e.g., Food and water, Shelter and safety, Belonging). The adaptation maps these layers to corresponding organizational "Culture" types (e.g., IMPULSIVE, DEPENDENT) on one side and specific workplace "Motivators" (e.g., Pay, Housing, Community, Prestige) on the other, creating a visual framework for alignment.

## Boundary conditions
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Does this business adaptation assume all employees progress sequentially through the hierarchy? (2) At what organizational "Culture" level is coaching as a leadership style most applicable?
