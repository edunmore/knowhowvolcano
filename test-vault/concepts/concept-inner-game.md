# Inner Game

## Definition
The Inner Game is a concept from Timothy Gallwey, indicating the player's internal state. It posits that "the opponent within one's own head is more formidable than the one the other side of the net." Its objective is to improve performance by reducing internal obstacles, thereby allowing natural learning and performance ability to emerge.

## Operationalization
The concept is operationalized through the "Inner Game Equation": Performance = potential – interference (P = p – i). This equation summarizes the objective of coaching: to improve performance (P) by growing potential (p) and decreasing interference (i).
