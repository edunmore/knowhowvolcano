# Coaching as the Mechanism for Adult Learning

## Rule
Coaching is the mechanism for transformation and adult learning in practice, essential for moving leadership style away from traditional command-and-control methods.

## Rationale
The source states that "coaching is the mechanism of transformation" needed to meet the modern need for self-belief and independence, which traditional command-and-control methods fail to do. It connects this to adult learning theory, which posits that adults learn differently from children, with self-belief being central, and asserts that "coaching is adult learning in practice." The text also suggests that a leader's failure to develop people often stems from only having learned by being told, implying coaching offers a different, more effective method.
