# Coaching Requires Expertise in Coaching, Not the Subject

## Rule
Coaching is not dependent on the coach being a more experienced person passing down their subject-matter knowledge. The expertise required is in the coaching process itself, which allows the coachee to go beyond the coach's own knowledge.

## Rationale
Passing down knowledge can undermine the building of self-belief, which creates sustained performance. Instead, effective coaching works from the coachee's own experience and perceptions. This is illustrated by the coach Mike Sprecklen, who, after exhausting his technical knowledge, found a new way forward by focusing on what the athletes could feel, enabling performance beyond his own limits.
