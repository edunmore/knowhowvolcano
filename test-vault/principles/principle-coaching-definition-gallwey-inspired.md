# Coaching Definition (Gallwey-Inspired)

## Rule
Coaching is unlocking people's potential to maximize their own performance. It is about helping them to learn, rather than teaching them.

## Rationale
This approach is grounded in the "Inner Game" concept, which posits that an individual's performance (P) is equal to their potential (p) minus interference (i). Coaching focuses on improving performance by growing potential and decreasing internal interference, as these internal obstacles are often more formidable than external ones. The method works by stimulating discovery from within, leveraging a person's built-in, natural learning capability, which can be disrupted by direct instruction.
