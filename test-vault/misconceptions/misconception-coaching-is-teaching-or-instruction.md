# Coaching is Teaching or Instruction

## Misconception
The misconception is that coaching is a form of teaching, instruction, training, or private tuition, where the coach's primary role is to impart knowledge, give technical input, and correct mistakes.

## Why it happens
This happens because common dictionary definitions, as noted in the source, conflate coaching with "sports instruction or training, private tuition, and extra teaching." This creates a widespread, but incorrect, association.

## Correction
Coaching is not about instruction or teaching. It is a future-focused journey where the coach helps the coachee discover answers from within by reducing internal obstacles (interference), thereby unlocking their natural potential. The coachee develops skills not by being told, but through this facilitated self-discovery within a powerful working relationship.
