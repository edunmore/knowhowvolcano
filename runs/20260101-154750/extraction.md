# Method Kernel

**Purpose**: [EXTRACTED] To provide a structured framework for guiding a coaching conversation to improve performance and learning by raising awareness and responsibility. (File 1, heading "9 The GROW Model", paragraph 1)
**Purpose**: [EXTRACTED] To transform intention into action through a sequence of questions. (File 1, heading "A framework for coaching", paragraph 2)

**Preconditions**: [EXTRACTED] The coach must possess the intention and skill to generate awareness and responsibility through active listening and powerful questioning. (File 1, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 1)
**Preconditions**: [EXTRACTED] The context must be one where coaching is appropriate, such as a formal session, an informal leadership conversation, or a team setting. (File 1, heading "Formal or informal?" and "One to one", paragraphs 1 & 1)

**Roles**:
*   **Coach**: [EXTRACTED] Facilitates the process by asking questions, listening actively, and holding the framework. Can be a leader, peer, or dedicated coach. (File 1, heading "One to one", paragraph 1)
*   **Coachee**: [EXTRACTED] The individual or team being coached, who provides answers, explores their own situation, and commits to actions. (File 1, heading "One to one", paragraph 1)

**Process**:
1.  **Goal**: [EXTRACTED] Begin by establishing what the coachee wants to achieve in the session and in the longer term. The core question is "What do you want?" (File 1, CONCEPT_DIAGRAM interpretation)
2.  **Reality**: [EXTRACTED] Explore the current situation, including actions taken so far and internal obstacles. The core question is "Where are you now?" (File 1, CONCEPT_DIAGRAM interpretation)
3.  **Options**: [EXTRACTED] Identify possible strategies and alternative courses of action. The core question is "What could you do?" (File 1, CONCEPT_DIAGRAM interpretation)
4.  **Will**: [EXTRACTED] Establish commitment to specific actions, timing, and accountability. The core question is "What will you do?" (File 1, CONCEPT_DIAGRAM interpretation)

**Loops/Order Changes**: [EXTRACTED] The sequence is flexible and recyclable. A vague initial Goal may need redefinition after exploring Reality. Options should be checked against the Goal. The final Will should be checked against the Goal and the coachee's motivation. (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 2)
**Loops/Order Changes**: [EXTRACTED] Coaching on an ongoing task may begin and end with any stage, not necessarily following the full G-R-O-W sequence. (File 1, heading "A framework for coaching", paragraph 3)

**Decision Rules**:
*   [EXTRACTED] Move to the next stage when the coachee has sufficient awareness and clarity on the current one. (File 1, heading "The key to GROW", paragraph 1)
*   [EXTRACTED] Revisit a previous stage if new information reveals a misalignment (e.g., an Option doesn't serve the Goal, or motivation for the Will is low). (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 2)
*   [INFERRED] The coach's intuition and instinct guide when to move or loop, rather than rigidly obeying a rule. (Anchors: File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 3; File 1, heading "The key to GROW", paragraph 1)

**Success Signals**: [EXTRACTED] The coachee sets an inspirational and stretching goal. (File 1, heading "The key to GROW", paragraph 1)
**Success Signals**: [EXTRACTED] The coachee demonstrates raised awareness and takes responsibility for their actions and decisions. (File 1, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 1)
**Success Signals**: [EXTRACTED] A clear plan of action with commitment and accountability is established. (File 1, heading "STEP 4: WHAT WILL YOU DO?", paragraph 1)

**Failure Modes & Fixes**:
*   **Failure**: [EXTRACTED] Using the framework autocratically (telling, not asking), which creates no awareness or real responsibility. (File 1, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 2)
    *   **Fix**: [EXTRACTED] Return to using powerful questions and active listening within each stage. (File 1, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 1)
*   **Failure**: [EXTRACTED] Setting goals based only on current reality, leading to uninspired, limited, or counterproductive aims. (File 1, heading "Goal first", paragraph 1)
    *   **Fix**: [EXTRACTED] Start with an ideal long-term vision, then set realistic steps toward it. (File 1, heading "Goal first", paragraph 2)
*   **Failure**: [EXTRACTED] Low self-motivation when committing to action (Will). (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 2)
    *   **Fix**: [EXTRACTED] Revisit the Goal to review and strengthen the coachee's sense of ownership. (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 2)

**Do/Don't Checklist**:
*   **DO**: [EXTRACTED] Spend sufficient time on Goal until it is inspirational. (File 1, heading "The key to GROW", paragraph 1)
*   **DO**: [EXTRACTED] Use the framework flexibly, recycling through stages as needed. (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 3)
*   **DO**: [EXTRACTED] Follow your coaching intuition. (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 3)
*   **DON'T**: [EXTRACTED] Treat the model as a rigid, linear sequence to be followed mechanically. (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 3)
*   **DON'T**: [EXTRACTED] Use the GROW acronym without the foundational context of awareness and responsibility. (File 1, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 1)

# Author Delivery Model

**Teaching Strategy**: [EXTRACTED] Presenting a core, simple mnemonic (GROW) as an accessible entry point, then layering on essential context and nuance to prevent misuse. (File 1, heading "A framework for coaching" and "More than GROW", paragraphs 1 & 1)
**Teaching Strategy**: [EXTRACTED] Using contrast to clarify concepts: formal vs. informal coaching, visionary vs. reactive goal-setting, coaching vs. autocratic telling. (File 1, headings "Formal or informal?", "Goal first", and "More than GROW")

**Persuasion Moves**:
*   [EXTRACTED] Asserting the model's effectiveness based on origin story and client success (e.g., development with McKinsey). (File 1, heading "Origins of the GROW Model", paragraphs 2-4)
*   [EXTRACTED] Framing the model as the "underpinning framework" for a successful coaching practice observed elsewhere. (File 1, heading "Origins of the GROW Model", paragraph 2)
*   [EXTRACTED] Underscoring the primacy of core principles (awareness, responsibility) over the model itself to establish credibility and depth. (File 1, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 1)

**Framing Contrasts**:
*   [EXTRACTED] **Coaching vs. Directive Leadership**: Coaching asks questions to generate awareness; autocratic telling does not. (File 1, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 2)
*   [EXTRACTED] **Vision-Led vs. Problem-Reactive Goals**: Goals from an ideal future are inspiring; goals from current reality are limiting. (File 1, heading "Goal first", paragraphs 1-2)
*   [EXTRACTED] **Flexible Tool vs. Rigid Rule**: GROW is a recyclable framework for intuition; it is not a strict linear sequence. (File 1, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 3)
