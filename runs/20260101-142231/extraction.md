# Method Kernel

### Purpose
[EXTRACTED] To structure coaching conversations to raise awareness and generate responsibility in the coachee, leading to improved performance and learning. (file 2, heading "A framework for coaching", paragraph 2)
[EXTRACTED] To serve as a flexible underpinning framework for coaching, whether formal or informal. (file 2, heading "Origins of the GROW Model", paragraph 4)

### Preconditions
[EXTRACTED] The coach must possess the intention and skill to generate awareness and responsibility through active listening and powerful questioning. (file 2, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] A coaching mindset is required, not an autocratic one. The model has little value without the context of awareness and responsibility. (file 2, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 3)
[INFERRED] A willing participant (coachee) and an environment conducive to dialogue are needed. (Anchors: file 2, heading "One to one", paragraph 1; file 1, heading "Active listening skills", paragraph 1)

### Roles
*   **Coach:** [EXTRACTED] Acts as a facilitator using questions and active listening. (file 2, heading "A framework for coaching", paragraph 2) [EXTRACTED] Maintains objectivity, detachment, and follows the coachee's agenda. (file 4, heading "Detachment", paragraph 1; file 4, heading "Follow the coachee", paragraph 1)
*   **Coachee:** [EXTRACTED] Owns the goals and the agenda for the conversation. (file 3, heading "The goal for the session", paragraph 1) [EXTRACTED] Provides answers, explores reality, and commits to action. (file 4, heading "Reality questions", paragraph 1)

### Process
**Core Sequence (GROW):**
1.  **Goal:** [EXTRACTED] Establish what the coachee wants (for the session and the issue). (file 2, Figure 9)
2.  **Reality:** [EXTRACTED] Explore the current situation objectively. (file 2, Figure 9)
3.  **Options:** [EXTRACTED] Identify possible strategies or courses of action. (file 2, Figure 9)
4.  **Will:** [EXTRACTED] Determine what will be done, when, by whom, and secure commitment. (file 2, heading "A framework for coaching", paragraph 2)

**Loops/Order Changes:**
[EXTRACTED] The sequence is flexible and recyclable. You may need to revisit earlier stages based on discoveries. (file 2, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] A vague initial Goal may need refinement after exploring Reality. (file 2, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 2)
[EXTRACTED] Options should be checked against the Goal. The Will should be checked against the Goal and motivation. (file 2, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 3)
[EXTRACTED] Move around the sequence according to intuition. (file 2, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 4)

### Decision Rules
[EXTRACTED] Spend sufficient time in Goal until it is inspirational and stretching. (file 2, heading "The key to GROW", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] Follow the coachee's interest/agenda; only raise omitted points when they are ready to leave an aspect. (file 4, heading "Follow the coachee", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] Use descriptive, not evaluative, terminology, especially in Reality. (file 4, heading "Description not judgment", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] Ask questions that compel the coachee to think, demand high-resolution focus, and seek descriptive answers. (file 4, heading "Reality questions", paragraph 1)

### Success Signals
[EXTRACTED] The coachee experiences raised awareness ("Eureka!" moments). (file 4, heading "Early resolution", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] The coachee takes ownership of goals and action steps. (file 3, heading "A sample coaching conversation", final paragraph)
[EXTRACTED] The coachee's self-motivation and commitment are high. (file 2, heading "Context and flexibility", paragraph 3)
[INFERRED] The conversation leads to clear, actionable next steps with accountability. (Anchors: file 2, heading "STEP 4: WHAT WILL YOU DO?", paragraph; file 3, heading "The goal for the session", paragraph 2)

### Failure Modes & Fixes
*   **Autocratic Use:** [EXTRACTED] The model is used to dictate, not question, creating no awareness or real responsibility. (file 2, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 3) *Fix:* Return to the core coaching mindset of generating awareness through questions.
*   **Rushing:** [EXTRACTED] Moving to Options prematurely before fully exploring Goal and Reality. (file 4, heading "Early resolution", paragraph 2) *Fix:* Dwell longer in Goal and Reality stages.
*   **Lack of Objectivity:** [EXTRACTED] Coach or coachee perception is distorted by judgment, prejudice, or false reality syndrome. (file 4, heading "Objectivity", paragraph 1; file 4, heading "Direct communication among peers", paragraph 3) *Fix:* Use descriptive language, manage own judgment, ask factual questions.
*   **Low Ownership:** [EXTRACTED] Goals are imposed, not chosen or agreed. (file 3, heading "Ownership of goals", paragraph 1) *Fix:* Coach for ownership even within constraints; ensure goals are agreed.

### Do/Don't Checklist
**Do:**
[EXTRACTED] Listen actively with full attention, using intuition. (file 1, heading "Be attentive to answers", paragraph 2)
[EXTRACTED] Ask open, powerful questions. (file 2, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] State goals in the positive. (file 3, heading "Positive focus", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] Follow the coachee's agenda. (file 4, heading "Follow the coachee", paragraph 1)
**Don't:**
[EXTRACTED] Prepare questions in advance while the coachee is speaking. (file 1, heading "Be attentive to answers", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] Give advice or share your own experience instead of listening. (file 1, heading "8 Active Listening", paragraph 2)
[EXTRACTED] Use judgmental or evaluative language. (file 4, heading "Description not judgment", paragraph 1)
[EXTRACTED] Assume you need to know all the facts; the coachee needs the clarity. (file 4, heading "Reality questions", paragraph 4)

### Framing Contrasts
*   **Coaching vs. Telling:** [EXTRACTED] Coaching generates awareness/responsibility; telling imposes solutions. (file 2, heading "More than GROW", paragraph 3)
*   **Goal-first vs. Reality-first:** [EXTRACTED] Goal-first inspires; reality-first limits. (file 2, heading "Goal first", paragraph 1)
*   **Description vs. Judgment:** [EXTRACTED] Description leads to objectivity; judgment leads to distortion. (file 4, Figure 11)
*   **Active Listening vs. Waiting to Speak:** [EXTRACTED] Active listening attends fully; common listening waits for a turn
