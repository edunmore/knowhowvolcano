*   **Process (Goal Setting - G)**:

    *   Involves distinguishing between various goal types: **Dream Goals** (vision, inspiration), **End Goals** (final objective, often outside full control), **Performance Goals** (measurable level of achievement, largely within control), and **Process Goals** (work needed, 100% within control).
        *   [EXTRACTED] (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, The goal for the issue, p1-2)
        *   [EXTRACTED] (017_10_g_goal_setting.md, From inspiration to action, p1)
    *   Goals should be **SMART** (Specific, Measurable, Agreed, Realistic, Timeframed), **PURE** (Positively stated, Understood, Relevant, Ethical), and **CLEAR** (Challenging, Legal, Environmentally sound, Appropriate, Recorded).

*   **Process (Reality Checking - R)**:
