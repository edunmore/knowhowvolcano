*   **One-page fable spec:**
    *   **Title:** The Mountain of Vision and the Path of Action
    *   **Characters:**
        *   **The Climber:** A well-intentioned but often self-limited individual, struggling to reach a peak.
        *   **The Guide:** A wise, questioning figure who never gives direct commands but always helps the Climber see clearly.
        *   **The Impassable Valley (Antagonist):** Represents a daunting problem or current reality.
        *   **The Whispers of Doubt (Internal Antagonist):** Represents self-limiting beliefs and negative assumptions.
    *   **Setting:** A grand mountain range with a distant, shimmering peak (Dream Goal) and a treacherous, fog-shrouded valley below (Current Reality).
    *   **Plot:**
        1.  **Opening:** The Climber gazes at the distant peak, feeling a mix of aspiration and overwhelming doubt about traversing the Valley.
        2.  **The Guide Appears:** The Guide asks, "What summit truly calls to you, and why?" (G - Dream Goal). The Climber describes a vague peak, shadowed by fears of the valley.
        3.  **Mapping the Fog:** The Guide then asks, "Where do your boots stand right now, and what exactly does this fog obscure?" (R - Reality). The Climber, prompted, describes the immediate terrain, the false paths taken, and the internal whispers of doubt.
        4.  **Finding New Trails:** The Guide, noticing the Climber's assumptions about the valley, asks, "If the fog lifted, or if you had wings, what paths *could* you take?" (O - Options). The Climber, encouraged, visualizes novel routes, some seemingly impossible at first.
