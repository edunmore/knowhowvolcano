Rule: Frame coaching goals as observable outcomes so progress can be tested.

Rationale: Observable framing reduces ambiguity, improves feedback loops, and supports concrete next actions.

Trade-offs: Over-specification can reduce motivation if the learner experiences it as rigid; some goals require qualitative indicators.

Examples (original): A manager does not aim for “more confidence”, but for “can deliver a 3-minute update without rushing and can handle 2 follow-up questions calmly.”
