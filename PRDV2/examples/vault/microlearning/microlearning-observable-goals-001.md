## Objective
Primary objective: [[objective-observable-goals]]

## Hook
A goal like “be more confident” feels clear until you try to measure progress.

## Core idea
Turn vague goals into observable indicators in a specific context so progress can be tested.

## Quick check
Rewrite: “I want better leadership.” Provide two observable indicators and one context detail.

## Tiny practice
Draft one coaching question that would help a client specify observable indicators.
