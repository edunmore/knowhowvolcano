## Definition
Learners can reframe a vague goal into a statement with observable indicators and context.

## Mastery criteria
- Produces at least 2 observable indicators.
- Includes context and a simple success condition.
- Avoids trait-only language without behaviors.

## Common misconceptions
- Treating goals as traits (“be confident”) instead of behaviors.
- Adding numbers without meaningful indicators.
