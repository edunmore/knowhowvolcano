# Coaching as Adult Learning

## Rule
Coaching helps people learn by unlocking their potential from within, rather than by instructing or teaching them. The focus is on removing internal obstacles to allow natural learning and performance to emerge.

## Rationale
This principle is grounded in the Inner Game concept, which posits that internal interference is a primary barrier to performance. The source states that humans have a "built-in, natural learning capability that is actually disrupted by instruction." Coaching stimulates discovery from within, which is more effective for adult learners than traditional teaching methods. This approach maximizes performance by growing potential and decreasing interference, as summarized in the Inner Game Equation: Performance = potential – interference.
