# Inner Game Equation

## Rule
Performance (P) is equal to potential (p) minus interference (i). To improve performance, focus on growing potential and decreasing interference.

## Rationale
The equation effectively summarizes the objective of modern coaching, which is to unlock a person's potential by reducing internal obstacles. If a coach helps remove or reduce internal obstacles to performance, a natural ability to learn and perform will emerge without needing much technical input.
