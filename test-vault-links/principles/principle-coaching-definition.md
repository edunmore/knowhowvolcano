# Coaching Definition

## Rule
Coaching is defined as unlocking a person's potential to maximize their own performance. It is a process focused on future possibilities, not past mistakes, and is about helping people to learn rather than teaching them.

## Rationale
This approach is effective because it leverages a person's natural, built-in learning capability, which is often disrupted by instruction. The method, inspired by the Inner Game, aims to improve performance by growing a person's potential and decreasing their internal interference or obstacles. The results are delivered largely through the powerful working relationship and the specific means and style of communication used, which stimulate the coachee to discover answers and develop skills from within.
