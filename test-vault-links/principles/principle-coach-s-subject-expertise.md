# Coach's Subject Expertise

## Rule
Coaching requires expertise in the process of coaching, not in the subject matter of the coachee. The coach must learn when to share their own knowledge and experience and when not to.

## Rationale
Sharing subject-matter expertise as a default approach undermines the building of the coachee's self-belief, which is necessary for sustained performance. Good coaching works from the coachee's experience and perceptions, allowing them to move beyond the limitations of the coach's own knowledge. This is a key strength of the coaching method.
