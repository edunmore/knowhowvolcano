# Coaching Core Components

## Definition
The Coaching Core Components are the essential elements that constitute the practice of coaching as described in the source. According to the text, coaching is fundamentally "about partnership, collaboration, and believing in potential." Its central premise is that "coaching and high performance come out of awareness and responsibility."

## Operationalization
The components can be observed through specific, named skills and a framework. The source explicitly identifies the "foundation coaching skills of powerful questioning and active listening" and a guiding "framework or compass for our coaching – the GROW model." These are the actionable elements that operationalize the principles of partnership and belief in potential.
