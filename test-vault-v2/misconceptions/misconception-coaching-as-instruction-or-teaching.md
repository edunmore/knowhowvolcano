# Coaching as Instruction or Teaching

## Misconception
The incorrect belief that coaching is synonymous with sports instruction, private tuition, or extra teaching, where the coach's role is to tell or teach the coachee facts and skills.

## Why it happens
The source text suggests this happens because common dictionary definitions (e.g., from Oxford Dictionaries) list coaching under the same category as "sports instruction or training" and "extra teaching," which reinforces the idea that coaching is about direct instruction.

## Correction
True coaching is not about instruction or teaching. It is a journey focused on future possibilities, where the coachee discovers new skills and behaviors "from within, stimulated by coaching." The coach's role is to build a powerful working relationship and use a specific style of communication to help the coachee reduce internal [[Interference]], thereby unlocking their natural potential for learning and performance, as illustrated by the [[Inner Game Equation]].
