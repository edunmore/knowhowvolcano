# Shifting from Dependence to Independence to Interdependence

## Rule
The primary function of coaching is to facilitate a shift from a state where an individual relies on external instruction to a state of self-reliance where they discover capabilities from within. This is achieved by focusing on the future, creating a powerful working relationship, and using a communicative style that helps the coachee reduce internal obstacles.

## Rationale
This shift works because it aligns with a person's natural learning capability, which is disrupted by direct instruction. The source text grounds this in the work of [[Timothy Gallwey]] and the [[Inner Game Equation]] (Performance = potential – interference). Coaching improves performance by helping individuals unlock their own potential and reduce their internal [[Interference]], rather than by providing technical input or teaching. This process of self-discovery fosters sustained performance improvement.
