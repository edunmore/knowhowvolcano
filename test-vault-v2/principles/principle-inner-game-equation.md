# Inner Game Equation

## Rule
The core rule for improving performance is expressed by the equation `Performance = potential – interference` (P = p – i). To improve performance, one must focus on growing an individual's inherent [[Potential]] while simultaneously decreasing their [[Interference]].

## Rationale
This principle works because [[Interference]], or the internal obstacles in one's own mind, is often more formidable than external challenges. According to [[Timothy Gallwey]], reducing this interference allows a person's natural ability to learn and perform to emerge spontaneously, often with minimal need for direct technical instruction from a coach. The equation effectively summarizes the objective of modern [[Coaching]], which is to unlock potential by helping people discover capabilities from within rather than through traditional teaching.
