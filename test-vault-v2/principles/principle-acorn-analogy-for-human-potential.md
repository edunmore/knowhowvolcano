# Acorn Analogy for Human Potential

## Rule
A wise leader, acting as a coach, should nurture an individual's inherent potential by providing nourishment and encouragement while carefully protecting their core, natural capabilities—their "tap root"—from being broken or disrupted by traditional, instructional methods.

## Rationale
The analogy posits that each person, like an [[acorn]], already contains all the potential to become a magnificent oak tree (i.e., achieve high performance). The source of [[Interference]] that hinders this growth is often well-intentioned but misguided instruction, which can disrupt natural learning, much like how transplanting a sapling without care for its delicate tap root sets back its development. Coaching, by contrast, focuses on unlocking this inherent potential by creating the right conditions for growth and preserving the individual's intrinsic capabilities, aligning with the [[Inner Game Equation]] (Performance = Potential – Interference).
