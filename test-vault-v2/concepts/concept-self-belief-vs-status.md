# Self-Belief (vs. Status)

## Definition
Self-belief, in the context of coaching, refers to the state where an individual's inherent [[potential]] for performance and learning is activated by removing or reducing [[Interference]]. This contrasts with an approach that relies on the external authority, instruction, or technical input of a coach, which can disrupt the learner's natural capability.

## Operationalization
Self-belief is operationalized as an increase in performance (P) as defined by the [[Inner Game Equation]] (P = p - i), where the reduction of internal obstacles (i) allows natural ability to emerge. It is observed when learning occurs through internal discovery stimulated by coaching, rather than through direct teaching or instruction.

## Boundary conditions
Not specified in this source. Open questions: (1) Does self-belief apply equally to all types of learning or performance tasks? (2) Are there individuals or contexts where reducing interference is insufficient, and some degree of external instruction is necessary?
