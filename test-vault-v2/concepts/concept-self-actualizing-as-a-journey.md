# Self-Actualizing (as a Journey)

## Definition
Self-actualizing is the highest state in a motivational hierarchy, representing the need for meaning and purpose. It is characterized by individuals whose work and activities are driven by a desire to contribute value to others, moving beyond proving themselves to themselves or others. The term is used to emphasize it is a never-ending journey, not a final, fixed destination.

## Boundary conditions
According to the source, self-actualizing emerges when both esteem needs (respect from others and [[self-belief]]) are satisfied, and the individual is no longer driven by the need for proof or external validation. It applies when an individual's primary motivator shifts to values and meaning, and their mindset becomes **interdependent**.
