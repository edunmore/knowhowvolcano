# Coaching (Modern Definition)

## Definition
**Coaching is unlocking people's potential to maximize their own performance.** It is a process that focuses on future possibilities, helping people learn by discovering from within rather than being taught. The process relies on a powerful working relationship and a specific style of communication to facilitate this self-discovery and improve performance.
